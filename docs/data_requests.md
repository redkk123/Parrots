## 1. Kapsetaki / Maley / Boddy: idade à morte e tecido por espécie de papagaio

**Para:** Amy M. Boddy <boddy@anth.ucsb.edu> (autora correspondente de Kapsetaki et al. 2024, *Evol Med Public Health* 12:105–116, doi:10.1093/emph/eoae011)
**Assunto:** Request: age at death and tumour site for parrot necropsies (Kapsetaki et al. 2024)

> Dear Dr Boddy, Dr Kapsetaki and colleagues,
>
> I am working on a comparative study of longevity and cancer in parrots (Psittaciformes). Your bird dataset (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.11311870) has been very useful: the budgerigar shows 20.8 % neoplasia prevalence (99/477) against 1.5 % in six lorikeet species (6/399), its closest relatives in the data.
>
> However, the cockatiel, also a domesticated species, shows 11.4 % (8/70), not significantly different from the budgerigar. To separate a lineage effect from domestication and age, I would be very grateful if you could share, for the 15 parrot species in the dataset and where available:
>
> 1. age at death (or age class) per necropsy, or at least per species summaries (median, IQR);
> 2. tumour site/tissue and benign vs malignant per case;
> 3. sex per case.
>
> Aggregated or de-identified data would be perfectly fine. I will cite the dataset and the article, and I am happy to share the analysis before any submission.
>
> With best regards,
> [nome, instituição]

## 2. Beaufrère / UC Davis: prevalência de neoplasia por gênero no banco UC Davis–Drury Reavill

**Para:** Hugues Beaufrère <hbeaufrere@ucdavis.edu> (autor correspondente de Megan et al. 2024, *J Avian Med Surg* 38(2), doi:10.1647/AVIANMS-D-24-00009; endereço da afiliação no PubMed)
**Assunto:** Request: neoplasia prevalence by psittacine genus in the UC Davis–Drury Reavill database

> Dear Dr Beaufrère and colleagues,
>
> Your study of adipocytic tumours in 26,013 psittacine submissions (11,737 necropsies) reports genus-level odds ratios, which is exactly the kind of denominator that is missing for parrot cancer comparisons.
>
> For a comparative study of longevity and cancer in parrots, I would like to ask whether it would be possible to obtain, from the same database and for necropsy cases:
>
> 1. number of necropsies and number with any neoplasm, by genus (or species where available), split into benign and malignant;
> 2. the same by age class and sex, if available;
> 3. tumour site for neoplastic cases.
>
> Genera of particular interest: *Melopsittacus*, *Nymphicus*, *Agapornis*, *Amazona*, *Psittacus*, *Ara*, *Cacatua*, *Eolophus*, lorikeets (*Trichoglossus*, *Eos*, *Lorius*) and *Neophema*. Aggregated counts are sufficient. I would cite the database and the article, and would welcome collaboration on any resulting analysis.
>
> With best regards,
> [nome, instituição]

## 1. Kapsetaki / Maley / Boddy: idade à morte e tecido por espécie de papagaio

**Para:** Amy M. Boddy <boddy@anth.ucsb.edu> (autora correspondente de Kapsetaki et al. 2024, *Evol Med Public Health* 12:105–116, doi:10.1093/emph/eoae011)
**Assunto:** Request: age at death and tumour site for parrot necropsies (Kapsetaki et al. 2024)

> Dear Dr Boddy, Dr Kapsetaki and colleagues,
>
> I am working on a comparative study of longevity and cancer in parrots (Psittaciformes). Your bird dataset (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.11311870) has been very useful: the budgerigar shows 20.8 % neoplasia prevalence (99/477) against 1.5 % in six lorikeet species (6/399), its closest relatives in the data.
>
> However, the cockatiel, also a domesticated species, shows 11.4 % (8/70), not significantly different from the budgerigar. To separate a lineage effect from domestication and age, I would be very grateful if you could share, for the 15 parrot species in the dataset and where available:
>
> 1. age at death (or age class) per necropsy, or at least per species summaries (median, IQR);
> 2. tumour site/tissue and benign vs malignant per case;
> 3. sex per case.
>
> Aggregated or de-identified data would be perfectly fine. I will cite the dataset and the article, and I am happy to share the analysis before any submission.
>
> With best regards,
> Angelo
